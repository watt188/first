# V9.9 Recovery Runtime Audit Gate

V9.9 adds a read-only convergence audit above the restart-safe V9.8 runtime.

## Purpose

Before another autonomous recovery step is allowed, the controller can inspect one exact-head logical action and determine whether durable state and journal evidence are idle, converged, awaiting reconciliation, blocked by an active foreign lease, or terminally aborted.

## Properties

- Exact-head binding is mandatory.
- The canonical V8.8 replay key is recomputed from PR/head/workflow/job/action/attempt.
- Durable V8.5 evidence and V8.9 lease integrity are verified before classification.
- PREPARED and COMMITTED-with-missing-evidence states are surfaced as reconciliation work rather than replayed.
- Active foreign leases are reported as blocked.
- ABORTED actions remain terminal.
- Legacy accepted evidence without a V9.3 journal record is reported read-only and never mutated.
- The auditor never calls the external recovery gateway and never changes durable state.

## Boundary

V9.9 is an observability and admission-control primitive, not a distributed consensus protocol. SQLite durability remains single-host. A later production controller can require `CONVERGED` or `HEALTHY_IDLE` before declaring recovery complete and route `*_NEEDS_RECONCILIATION` states back through V9.8.
